ContactFusion investigates how a robot can improve its representation of the environment by physically interacting with it.

The project combines visual observations with contact measurements to update a probabilistic geometric map. Contact provides local geometric evidence in regions that may be occluded or poorly observed by vision.

**Role in my research:** representation

**Topics:** probabilistic geometry · contact sensing · mapping · implicit surfaces
